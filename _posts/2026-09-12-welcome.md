SAFER Lab (Safety Analytics for Equitable & Resilient mobility) is the new research home of [SR4A](https://sr4a.com), Safety and Resilience for All. The lab brings together our peer-reviewed work on weather-related crash risk, crash severity modeling and automated-vehicle readiness, and will grow with every conference season.

Our publications are listed on the [research](../research) page and are pulled automatically from ORCID, and our research themes are laid out under [Research Themes](../projects). If you would like to collaborate, [get in touch](../contact).
