SAFER Lab (Safety Analytics for Equitable & Resilient mobility) is the new research home of [SR4A](https://sr4a.com), Safety and Resilience for All. The lab runs community-based programs that prevent road injuries among children, young people and vulnerable road users, pilots technology that makes cities more livable, and helps communities stay connected through floods and extreme weather — each one designed with the people it serves and evaluated so partners and funders can see what worked.

Our program areas are laid out under [Programs](../projects), and evaluations, reports and publications will appear on the [research](../research) page as they are released. If you would like to partner with us or fund a program, [get in touch](../contact).
