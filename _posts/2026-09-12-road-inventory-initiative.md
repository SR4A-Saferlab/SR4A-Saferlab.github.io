SAFER Lab's first project is the [Nigeria Road Inventory Initiative](../projects/road-inventory): a comprehensive, georeferenced record of the road network and everything on it, from lane counts and surface condition to crossings, lighting, speed limits and traffic mix.

Nigeria does not currently have this data in any usable form, which means agencies plan without knowing what is out there and researchers cannot model where risk is highest. We are starting with a pilot in a single local government area, building a complete local dataset and the collection methods to go with it, and scaling from there toward national coverage. The data will be published openly.

We are looking for a pilot local government partner, collaborators in road asset data and GIS, and funders. If that is you, [get in touch](../contact).
