Abim is a Traffic/ITS Engineer and the founding director of SAFER Lab. He holds a Ph.D. in Transportation Systems from the University of North Carolina at Charlotte, where his research developed and validated machine learning systems for weather-related crash prediction, integrating geospatial analytics with advanced machine learning for crash risk mapping, severity prediction and forecasting.

He has collaborated with the U.S. Department of Transportation through the University Transportation Centers program, and currently practices as a Traffic/ITS Engineer at Gresham Smith supporting Georgia Department of Transportation programs in traffic forecasting, safety analysis and signal operations. He was named an ITE Young Leader to Follow in 2026 and a Lifesavers Traffic Safety Scholar in 2024.
